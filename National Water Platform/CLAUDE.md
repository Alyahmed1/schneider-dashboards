# Egypt National Water Platform: project notes

Own Vite + React + TypeScript app (port 4741) in the schneider-dashboards repo, folder `National Water Platform/`. One national shell over the whole water sector: cover, sidebar, header, alerts bell, account menu (roles), and a Home screen that rebuilds `Egypt National Water Platform Dashboard.png` in code. All data is simulated.

## Shared data (src/data.ts, one source for every page)
17.5 M m3/day supply, ~115 M people, 19.8 M capacity, reuse 4.1 M = 23% (3.3 to agriculture + 0.8 to industry/landscape), irrigation efficiency +15%, emissions -12%. MWRI 9.7 M acres irrigated; MoALR 810 K acres smart-managed.

## Open decision (Aly, 2026-11-10)
On very wide windows (e.g. 1907x857) the stage stays 16:9 and a darker strip shows left and right. Aly agreed to leave it for now and DECIDE IN PHASE 5 whether to rebuild the layout to stretch to the full width (would need re-checking every page at many window shapes).

## Layout rule
The whole UI is drawn on a 1600 x 900 stage and scaled to fit (`ui.tsx`: `useScale`, contain, never stretched). Sizes in CSS are design pixels, so no vw/vh inside the stage. Every page must fit one screen.
