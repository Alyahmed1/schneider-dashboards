# Pivot Monitoring dashboard: project notes

Own Vite app (port 4740) in the schneider-dashboards repo, folder `monitoring water/`. Monitoring and control of 4 center pivots, built mainly for a tablet (exact inch size still to come). Look, theme and motion copy the other water dashboards (`../water irrigation`): green accent on deep navy-teal panels, dark only. Data is simulated.

## Agreed with Aly (2026-11-10)
- 2 tabs. Tab 1 "Pivots": the farmland picture with 4 pivot circles in a fixed 2x2 layout, a chip under each (name, state, field water %, m3 today) and fleet totals on top; tapping a circle opens Tab 2. Tab 2 "Pivot detail": recreation of `Schneider Electric Irrigation Dashboard.png` (reference only, do not use it as a background). Electricity Price is replaced by **Water Consumption** (day/night bars, m3/h, with a daily target line). Units are metric (my guess, Aly can switch back to MPH and inches).
- Pivots are the first four of Smart Irrigation: P1 Wheat, P2 Wheat, P3 Maize, P4 Maize (P4 has the Tower 7 drive fault), 150 feddan each (`src/pivots.ts`).
- Background `public/bg.jpg` = "Geometric Irrigated Fields at Dusk" (Aly generated), dimmed with a vignette, on both tabs.
- A symbol on every widget, KPI, button, nozzle, state badge and chart label.
- "Ask the assistant" button (port from irrigation `Assistant.tsx` and `askengine.ts`, scoped to the current pivot), fixed spot in the header, Phase 3.
- Before commit/push/deploy: Aly approves it locally. Push goes to the schneider-dashboards repo; warn first (pushing creates canceled records on the other Vercel projects); deploy by hand with `vercel deploy --prod` in this folder (new Vercel project).

## Layout rule (everything fits, nothing overlaps, nothing stretched)
`stage.ts`: the dashboard is drawn on a design canvas and scaled as a whole (contain, never stretched). Landscape: 800 high x 1066..1440 wide; portrait: 800 wide x 1066..1440 high. Sizes in CSS are design pixels, so do not use vw/vh inside the stage. Panels use `min-width/min-height: 0` and `overflow: hidden`; touch targets are at least 40px. Each drawing measures its box and lays itself out on a viewBox of the same shape.
Check at 1024x768, 1280x800, 1366x1024, 1920x1200 and portrait 768x1024 (`?debug` shows the stage size).
